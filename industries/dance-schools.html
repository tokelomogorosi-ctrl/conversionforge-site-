FAQ

Questions people ask

Did you build HK Dance Studios' website?

No. Her website was built by another provider. Our work for HK Dance Studios is the Facebook and Instagram adverts and their measurement.

Do I need a new booking system?

No. We keep the one you use and point the website and the adverts to it.

How much should I spend on adverts?

Start small, in one town with room in its classes, and set a stop line before you spend. Your ad spend goes straight to Meta; our management is part of the monthly plan.

Can you put the photographs of the children online?

Only with the parents' consent recorded, and only for the uses they agreed to. We ask before we use any picture.

What does a timetable website cost?

A website you own starts from £175, and most go live in under 72 hours. Care and hosting after launch is from £50 a month, with no VAT added.
