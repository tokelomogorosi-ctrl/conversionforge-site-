FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I have to be on camera?

No. Most of what works for a restaurant is the food, the grill and the room. If you want to be in it, we film you; if not, we film the plates.

Will you post anything without asking me?

No. Every post is sent to you first, and nothing goes out without your yes.

How soon will I see results?

Views move within the first weeks. Bookings and orders take longer, which is why we measure them separately and say which is which.

Can I take orders without a delivery app?

Yes. Your own ordering page takes collection pre-orders through your own card system, and you can keep the apps alongside it.
