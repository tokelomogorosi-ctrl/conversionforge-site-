FAQ

Questions people ask

Do you charge VAT?

No. Conversion Forge is not VAT registered, so no VAT is added to any price. The price you see is the price you pay.

Why hours?

Because it is honest about what you are buying. Every plan is priced at the same £50 an hour, and the monthly report shows where each hour went.

Is ad spend included?

No. Ad spend is paid by you straight to Meta or Google, so you can see every penny. Managing the adverts is part of Furnace and Full Forge.

Can I change plan?

Yes. Move up or down as the business needs; we agree the change in writing before the next month starts.

Is there a minimum term?

The terms are written down with the price before we start. Ask and we will show you them first.
