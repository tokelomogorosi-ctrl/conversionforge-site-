Service

Where small-business leads actually come from

For a local business the leads come from three places, and none of them is a mystery. The Google Business Profile, where a search turns into a call, a direction request, a message or a booking. Your own website, with a form or a booking link that lands in your inbox. And outreach, sent on a fixed cadence, where every new thread starts with a human saying yes.

I build all three so they work while you are working. The systems are yours when they are done.

The honest limitation first: a lead is not a customer until they turn up. I measure to the booking, and I ask for your turn-up numbers, because that is where most small businesses leak.

Capture on your own site, not a rented form

The form lives on your domain and delivers to your inbox or your phone. The list is yours. If a platform changes its terms or its prices, your leads do not go with it.

There is nothing to log into and nothing to renew. A lead arrives as an email or a message, the way you already work.

Follow-up that happens without you

Most leads go cold because nobody wrote back a second time. The follow-up sequence runs on days 3, 7, 14 and 21, and it stops the moment someone replies.

Every message passes a plain-language check, so it reads like a person wrote it, because a person did. Sends are spaced so no inbox provider flags them. Nothing goes out on a new thread without a human approving the first message.

The part most agencies skip: what happens after the lead

A lead with nowhere to go is a phone number in a spreadsheet. So I build the step after the lead as part of the system.

For Jerkeez 876 that step is pre-orders for collection on jerkeez.co.uk. For Pilates Realm it is a booking flow on pilatesrealm.com. For Chaplins Barbers it is booking by WhatsApp from chaplinsofsevenoaks.co.uk. In each case the lead has a next action that the business can fulfil the same day.

A limitation, stated early

I can build the capture, the follow-up and the booking step. I cannot make people turn up. I measure to the booking and ask you for the turn-up numbers, and if the gap between the two is wide, that is the next thing we fix, together, not something I hide in a report.
