01

The short answer

The minimum for a small business website with us is £175. That buys a simple site, and most service businesses are live in under 72 hours. Most websites cost more than the minimum, depending on the pages and what the site has to do. It is a real starting price, written down, not a figure you only hear after a call.

If you also want to be found properly on Google, the Starter package is £495. It includes the website, your Google Business Profile set up, the local SEO foundations and 30 days of support. The Growth System is £995 and adds lead capture, email follow-up sequences, a monthly performance report and 90 days of support. Bigger automation projects are priced per project, after the free audit.

02

What changes the price

Three things move a website up from the £175 minimum.

The first is size. A one-page site for a trade or a studio is quicker to build than a site with a page for every service and every town you cover.

The second is what the site has to do. Taking bookings, showing a menu with pre-orders, or selling products online all add work. A full online shop takes about two weeks rather than three days.

The third is the words and the pictures. If you send us your own photographs and the facts about your business, the site comes together faster. If the words need writing from scratch, that takes longer, and we tell you before we start.

03

What you own, and what it costs to keep

You own the code, the content and the domain. No monthly fee is needed to keep the site live, and if you ever want someone else to work on it, you take it with you.

Hosting for a site like this can cost nothing at all, because the pages are fast static files. Your domain name renews once a year with the registrar, in your name.

If you want help after launch, ongoing care plans start from £35 a month, and social media management starts from £300 a month. Both are optional, and you can stop whenever you like.

04

What we do not charge for

The free audit costs nothing and comes with no obligation. You get a plain look at how your business shows up when someone searches for it, what is costing you enquiries, and what to fix first. You keep that plan whether we work together or not.

The search basics are not an upsell either. Every site we build ships with a sitemap, a robots file, schema markup and the llms.txt files that help AI assistants describe your business correctly.
