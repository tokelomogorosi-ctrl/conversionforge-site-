Why the ownership matters more than the design

Most small-business websites are rented. The agency holds the domain, the code sits on their platform, and the monthly fee is what keeps the lights on. Stop paying and the site goes dark. That arrangement suits the agency, not the business.

I build the site once and hand it over. You own the code, the content and the domain, and no retainer is required to keep it live. If you ever want someone else to work on it, you take it with you.

There is no account manager here. Conversion Forge is one person, me, Tk, working from Sevenoaks in Kent. The person you email is the person who builds the site.

02

What every site I build ships with, on day one

The search foundations are not an upgrade. They ship with the site the day it goes live, because a site nobody can find is a brochure in a drawer.

Every site carries a sitemap.xml, a robots.txt that names all 15 AI and search crawlers by name, and llms.txt plus llms-full.txt so an AI assistant asked about your business can read the facts straight from you. Every page carries JSON-LD schema: the business, the breadcrumbs, and the questions and answers where a page has them. Open Graph and Twitter cards are set so a shared link shows a proper card. Canonical tags are set so the right page gets the credit.

Images carry their dimensions, which is a small thing that stops them vanishing on iPhones. Legal pages are included. The build is mobile-first, because that is where most of your visitors are.

Before any site goes live it passes a 20-check ship gate. I wrote that gate from real client incidents, one check per thing that once went wrong, so the same mistake cannot ship twice.

What this is not

I do not build large e-commerce platforms, and I do not build apps with logins. If that is what you need, I am not the right fit and I will tell you early rather than late.

What I build is fast, owned sites for businesses that need to be found and booked. A restaurant that wants pre-orders. A barber that wants WhatsApp bookings. A trade that wants its work seen at full size on a phone.

There is no monthly fee to keep your site live. Care plans exist for people who want someone on hand, and they are optional.
