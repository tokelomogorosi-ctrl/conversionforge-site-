04

SEO in Sevenoaks: what we change

Local SEO for a Sevenoaks business starts with the words Google reads first. The town goes into the page titles and the headings, and the business schema carries your real address and the towns you serve. The sitemap carries true dates, and canonical tags settle which page gets the credit.

Then the Google Business Profile, because for a Sevenoaks search the map is where most customers decide. The finished profile has the right categories, the services listed one by one, the real photographs, weekly posts, reviews from real customers, answered, and the booking or ordering link.

Rankings take weeks to move, and nobody can promise you a position. What we promise is the work, the measurement, and a Search Console report you can check yourself. The method in full is on the local SEO page.

Leads, once people find you

Being found is half of it. The other half is somewhere for the search to go: Chaplins books by WhatsApp, Jerkeez takes pre-orders for collection, and the enquiry lands in the owner's own inbox or phone.

Follow-up sequences run on days 3, 7, 14 and 21 and stop the moment someone replies. That is the lead generation system, and if you want paid ads as well, they are measured in bookings, not clicks.
