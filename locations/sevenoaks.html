Three Sevenoaks businesses and what changed

7 Oaks Grill is a restaurant with strong word of mouth and, until recently, no online presence. An Instagram content system and Google Business Profile work reached 28,000+ accounts and produced 71,583 views in 29 days, with zero ad spend. The numbers are the platforms' own.

Chaplins Barbers, at 41 Dartford Road, is a heritage barber built around the year 1889. Bookings moved to WhatsApp and the shop got its first proper website at chaplinsofsevenoaks.co.uk, handed over for a single fee.

Jerkeez 876, at 73 London Road, serves Jamaican and Caribbean food. The menu and pre-orders for collection are online at jerkeez.co.uk. The 876 is Jamaica's dialling code, the kind of detail I look for before writing a line.

What a Sevenoaks business gets from me

A website you own from the first day, with the search and AI-search foundations already in place: a sitemap, a robots file naming all 15 AI crawlers, schema on every page, and an llms.txt so the assistants people now ask for recommendations can read your facts in your words.

Copy written from your business, in plain British English, with the street and the specifics in it. A booking or ordering step that fits how you already work, WhatsApp for a barber, pre-orders for a kitchen.

And a Google Business Profile finished properly, because for a Sevenoaks search the map is where most customers decide.
