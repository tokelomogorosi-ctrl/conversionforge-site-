Twenty-one miles from Charing Cross

Conversion Forge is one person, working from Sevenoaks, 21 miles from Charing Cross in a straight line. Close enough to walk into a London shop and look at it properly. Far enough that you are dealing with the person who builds the site, not an account team on the fourth floor.

The honest limitation first: I am one person, so I take on work I can deliver well, not every London brief that arrives.

02

A Bromley studio, top five percent of its category

Bromley is a London borough, and Persa London makes hand-painted leather bags, bracelets and accessories there, each piece by one hand. The eBay listings were rewritten from keyword research, 25+ of them, and the catalogue rebuilt into 240 product variations.

The result, from eBay's own figures: the top 5 percent of the UK category, 61,515 impressions in 30 days and a 35.9 percent improvement in conversion rate. The brand now sells from its own shop at persalondon.co.uk as well.

03

What a London business gets from me

A website you own, handed over with the domain, the content and the code. Search foundations from the first day: a sitemap, a robots file naming all 15 AI crawlers, schema on every page and an llms.txt, so both Google and the AI assistants people ask for recommendations can read your facts.

A booking or ordering step that fits how you already work, and a site that passes a 20-check gate before it goes live.

04

Found borough by borough

A London customer rarely searches for "London". They search for the thing they want and the place they are standing: a barber near them, a caterer in their borough, a fitter who covers their postcode.

So the work is local on purpose. Your borough and the areas you actually serve go into your titles, your headings and your business schema, and your Google Business Profile carries the same facts. Nobody can promise a position, and I will not; what I promise is the work and a Search Console report you can check yourself.
