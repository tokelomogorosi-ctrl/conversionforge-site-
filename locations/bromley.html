A studio in Bromley, sold everywhere

Persa London makes hand-painted leather and canvas bags, bracelets and accessories, each piece by one hand, from a studio in Bromley. Persian heritage, London craft, in their own words.

The work was already good. What the studio lacked was a marketplace presence that matched it. This page is about what changed, and what a Bromley business can expect from the same approach.

The honest limitation first: I cannot promise any shop a position in its category. The result below came from listing work done properly and measured by the platform, not from a guarantee.

02

What was rebuilt, and the numbers after

On eBay, the listings were rewritten from keyword research, 25+ of them, and the catalogue was rebuilt to 240 product variations so every size, colour and finish had its own place to be found. Seller-rating hygiene brought the account to Above Standard with zero defects.

The result, from eBay's own figures: the top 5 percent of the UK category, 61,515 impressions in 30 days, and a 35.9 percent improvement in conversion rate.

03

From marketplace to a shop she owns

A marketplace shop is rented space. The studio now has its own shop at persalondon.co.uk, so the brand is no longer only a tenant. The marketplace still brings people in. The website is where the brand is itself.

04
