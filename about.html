About

One person.
Sevenoaks, Kent.

Conversion Forge is not an agency floor. It is me, Tokelo Mogorosi, and a stack of systems I built so that a small business can get the same machinery a big one buys, without the monthly bill.

Who you are dealing with

There is no account manager.
There is just me.

My name is Tokelo Mogorosi. I was born in South Africa and I live in Sevenoaks. I started Conversion Forge after I lost my job in December 2025, which is a plain thing to say and a hard thing to live through. What I had was time, a stubborn habit of taking systems apart to see how they run, and a suspicion that most small businesses were paying agencies a lot of money for work that could be built once and handed over.

That suspicion turned out to be right, and it is now the whole business.

When you talk to Conversion Forge, you are talking to me. I answer the first message, I run the discovery call, I build the thing, and I am the one you shout at if it breaks. There is no team to hide behind and no junior learning on your money.

The work

Built once. Handed over.
Yours to keep.

Most agencies rent you a website. Stop paying, and it goes dark. I build the system, put your name on it, and give you the keys: the code, the accounts, the passwords, the lot. If you never speak to me again, everything I built keeps running.

The work itself is unglamorous and specific. Getting a business to show up on Google Maps when someone nearby is searching for it. Building a site that turns a visitor into a booking instead of an award. Rewriting an eBay listing so it actually gets found. Setting up the outreach that fills a quiet week.

I use a lot of automation to do it, which is why the prices are what they are. That is the honest reason. It is not magic and I am not going to dress it up as magic.

Who I have built for

Real businesses.
Most of them down the road.

A barber shop in Sevenoaks. A personal trainer. A car dealer. A Jamaican restaurant. A jewellery seller doing 240 product variations on eBay. These are not logos I bought the right to display. They are people whose sites I built and whose numbers I can show you, and several of them are a short walk from my front door.

The numbers on the results page come from the platforms that produced them, not from a spreadsheet I made up. If a figure cannot be traced back to Search Console, eBay Seller Hub, or Instagram Insights, it does not go on the page.

Some of the work did not go to plan, and where that happened I say so on the case study rather than quietly leaving it out. An agency that has never had a bad month is an agency that is lying to you.

How I work

Four things I will not move on.

You own it. Every engagement ends with a handover folder. Source code, content, accounts, credentials. No hostage situations.

No retainer unless you want one. You pay for a thing, you get the thing. If you want me to keep improving it afterwards, that is a separate conversation and it is your choice, not a condition.

Every number is traceable. If I cannot show you where a figure came from, I will not use it.

I tell you when something will not work. Including when the thing you are asking me to sell you is the wrong thing to buy. I would rather lose the sale than build you something that quietly fails for a year.
