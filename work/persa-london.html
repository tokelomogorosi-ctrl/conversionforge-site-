01

The business before

Persa London makes hand-painted leather and canvas bags, bracelets and accessories from a studio in Bromley, London. Each piece is made by one hand. Persian heritage, London craft, in the brand's own words.

The product was already the kind that sells itself in person. On eBay it was harder to find than it deserved to be, and the listings did not carry the words a buyer would actually type.

02

What was rebuilt

The listings, first. More than 25 of them were rewritten from keyword research, so a search for a hand-painted leather bracelet found one. The catalogue was rebuilt into 240 product variations, so every colour, size and finish had its own place in the results rather than hiding inside one listing.

The photography got direction, so the pieces were shown the way they are seen in the hand. And the account's seller rating was tidied to Above Standard with zero defects, because a marketplace shows the well-run shop first.

03

The numbers, from eBay

The shop reached the top 5 percent of its UK category. In 30 days the listings drew 61,515 impressions, and the conversion rate improved by 35.9 percent.

The figures are eBay's own. A marketplace reports what it sees, and what it saw was a shop that had become easy to find and easy to buy from.

04

From marketplace to a shop she owns

A marketplace shop is rented space, and the terms belong to the landlord. Persa London now has its own shop at persalondon.co.uk. The marketplace still brings buyers in. The website is where the brand is fully itself, on a domain it owns.

05

What we would do next

The next number is repeat purchase. A brand where each piece is made by one hand has a story that keeps customers, and the site is the place to tell it, with the pieces photographed at full size and the making shown. We would build towards customers who come back to the shop she owns rather than back to the marketplace.
