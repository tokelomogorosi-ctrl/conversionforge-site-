Not promises. Numbers from the platforms.

Eight case studies: a Sevenoaks grill with 71,583 views in 29 days, a Bromley leather brand in the top 5 percent of its eBay category, a barber, a restaurant, a fitness coach, a Pilates instructor, an academy and a fitter.
