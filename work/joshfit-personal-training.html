01

The business before

Joshfit Personal Training has been running since 2014 from Phoenix Leisure Centre in Bradwell, Great Yarmouth. Personal training, nutrition and diet plans, online classes and morning circuits, run by a competitive bodybuilder who trains the way he coaches.

Twelve years of clients and results, and no front door online that matched them. A business that had grown on reputation needed a page that could carry it to someone who had never been in the gym.

02

What was built

A full site at joshfitpt.co.uk, built from the business's own facts: the services, the setting, the years. A merchandise catalogue, so the kit the clients already wanted had a place to be bought. And a second version with personal-training extras, because the first version showed what visitors were looking for.

The copy names Bradwell and Great Yarmouth, because a personal trainer is found by town, and the site is built to be found that way.

03

The domain, moved and owned

The domain was moved to a registrar the client controls and the site was handed over with it. That is the part most people never check until they want to leave a provider and find they cannot.

Joshfit owns its domain, its site and its content. No retainer keeps it live.

04

Delivered from Kent to Norfolk

The whole build was delivered remotely from Sevenoaks to Great Yarmouth. Facts gathered by message, drafts reviewed on a phone, the domain moved without anyone travelling.

Distance changed nothing about the work or the ownership. It is the same build as one for a business on the next street.

05

What I would do next

Measure enquiries, then bookings. A trainer's site has one job, which is to turn a reader into a first session, and the next step is a booking step that can be counted and a Google Business Profile that carries it. After that, the catalogue's own numbers, so the kit that sells guides what gets stocked.
